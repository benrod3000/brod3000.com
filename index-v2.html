How It's Built

The decisions behind a production-grade product.

Provider abstraction

SES, SendGrid, and Resend behind a single interface. No vendor lock-in. Swap providers without touching application code.

Queue system

Upstash Redis for reliable delivery at scale. Jobs retry with exponential backoff across providers. Not a polling loop.

Auth & trust

OAuth (Google/GitHub), Cloudflare Turnstile, disposable-email filtering, 2FA/TOTP, and audit logs. Built for production from day one.

This isn't a CRUD app with a Mailchimp integration. Every component was designed for reliability, not speed to market.

How I Work

Systems over tactics

The queue retries with exponential backoff. The infrastructure handles failure, not the campaign.

Audience ownership

Every subscriber has a timeline. The platform treats audiences as relationships, not data points.

Compound retention

Analytics measure LTV, not vanity opens. Acquisition and retention share the same architecture.
