The Problem

Businesses spend years building audiences on platforms they don't control.

Followers disappear overnight

A platform algorithm change can cut your reach by 80%. You don't own the relationship. You rent it.

Algorithms change without warning

The rules you built your growth on can vanish. Your distribution is not yours to control.

Customer relationships get fragmented

Email here, social there, ads somewhere else. No single source of truth for who your audience actually is.

You're building your business on rented land. When the landlord changes the rules, your growth disappears.

The businesses that win the next decade will own their audience relationships, their customer data, and their distribution.

04

Product Philosophy

How I think about building products.

01

Systems Over Tactics

"I don't optimize campaigns. I build the infrastructure that makes campaigns work."

Campaigns are temporary. Infrastructure compounds. Every system I build is designed to get stronger the longer it runs, not weaker.

02

Audience Ownership

"Your audience is your most valuable business asset. Treat it that way."

Platforms treat audiences like inventory. I build tools that treat them like relationships. The difference shows up in every product decision.

03

Compound Retention

"Acquisition creates attention. Retention creates value."

LTV compounds when acquisition and retention share the same architecture. Most tools optimize for one or the other. I build for both.
